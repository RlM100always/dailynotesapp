# 🗒️ DailyNotes - Easy Notepad

**DailyNotes - Easy Notepad** is a smart, lightweight, and beautifully crafted note-taking Android application to help you stay organized and productive! Whether you're jotting down quick thoughts, organizing projects, or setting reminders — DailyNotes makes it effortless. ✨

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📱 User Features

- ✅ **Add, Update, Delete & Archive Notes**:  
  Create notes instantly, edit them anytime, and archive or delete with a single tap.

- 🎨 **Color-Coded Notes**:  
  Stay visually organized by assigning colors to notes for easier categorization.

- 🗂️ **Multiple Folders**:  
  Group your notes into folders like Personal, Work, Travel, etc.

- 🔄 **Auto Backup**:  
  Your notes are always safe with automatic local backups.

- 📤 **Export Notes (PDF, HTML, TXT, JSON)**:  
  Share your notes in multiple formats with just one click.

- 🌗 **Light & Dark Mode**:  
  Switch between light and dark themes to suit your reading comfort.

- ⚙️ **Dynamic Settings**:  
  Customize font size, note view layout, and other UI preferences.

- 🖋 **Text Formatting & Multimedia Support**:  
  Use **bold**, _italic_, and _underline_ along with image/file attachments to enrich your notes.

- ⏰ **Alarm & Reminder Feature**:  
  Set reminders for individual notes — never miss important tasks again!

---

## 🛠 Technology Stack

- **Programming Language**: Kotlin ☕  
- **UI Design**: XML 🎨  
- **Architecture**: MVVM 🏗️  
- **Local Storage**: Room Database (SQLite) 🗃️
